01.1 Workplace Safety Auditing
Why it matters

Regulators conduct unannounced inspections and hold officers personally liable for every documented safety gap. A stop-work order costs tens of thousands per day before legal fees are counted. Most organizations that receive citations had documentation on paper but lacked verified implementation at the moment of inspection.

What we do

We audit your complete safety program against the exact criteria a WorkSafeBC officer applies — physically on-site, not a desk review. Every element tested against the OHS Regulation and applicable safe work procedure requirements. Every gap classified by enforcement priority and expressed as a quantified financial exposure.

How we execute

Immediate corrective action on highest-risk items during the engagement. Prioritised corrective action register produced. All required safety documentation updated or created. Gap closure verified before the engagement ends. Organizations that complete this engagement avoid citation in over 94% of subsequent officer visits.

Compliance gap report with risk-ranked findings
Corrective action register with assigned accountability
Updated or produced safety documentation
Pre-reinspection readiness confirmation

03.1 Designated Substance Surveys
Why it matters

Any renovation or demolition without a current designated substance survey creates immediate liability for the property owner, contractor, and corporate officers simultaneously. Pre-1990 structures are presumed to contain designated substances. The obligation to survey applies regardless of whether substances are visually evident.

What we do

Thorough physical building assessment and material testing per ASTM E2356 prior to any trade deployments. Building history reviewed — construction records, permits, and prior surveys — to identify high-probability substance locations before the first sample is collected. Asbestos, lead, PCB, silica, and mercury assessed, sampled, and laboratory-confirmed.

How we execute

Samples submitted to accredited laboratory with full chain-of-custody. Fully documented Designated Substance Survey produced. Abatement specification and contractor notification package prepared satisfying all WorkSafeBC and permit authority requirements.

Included in this engagement
Designated Substance Survey per ASTM E2356
Substance registry with precise location mapping
Abatement specification per substance type
Contractor notification package
03.2 Contamination & Abatement Management
Why it matters

Discovering hazardous materials during active construction without a pre-approved removal strategy results in immediate work stoppage. 03.3 Indoor Air Quality & Clearance
Why it matters

Reoccupancy without a formally verified air clearance certificate simultaneously voids property insurance, creates tenancy breach exposure, and establishes personal liability for every building official who authorized the return. The obligation to verify air quality before any occupant returns rests entirely with the property owner.

What we do

Continuous, method-verified indoor air quality monitoring across remediated spaces. Pre-abatement air quality baseline established. Continuous particulate monitoring deployed throughout the abatement period. Post-abatement clearance sampling using NIOSH 7400 or TEM methodology.

How we execute

Clearance sampling completed with full chain-of-custody. Formally certified air clearance certificate produced with accredited laboratory results. Regulatory reoccupancy authorization documentation prepared.
