About us

The firm.

Two principals. 35 years of combined experience. One integrated firm built to navigate operational risk.

The name comes from the navigators who mapped unknown coastlines with incomplete information and high stakes. That is exactly what compliance looks like in a complex regulatory environment.

Wayfinder EHS Consulting is an occupational health, safety, and environmental consulting firm based in Canada, operating nationally and internationally. We embed alongside client teams, map their complete exposure terrain, and deliver sequenced, costed outcomes with independently verifiable results.

Our practice spans three domains — OHS, Environmental & ESG, and Building Sciences — delivered under a single engagement model. No handoffs. No gaps between disciplines.
