About Wayfinder

Who Are The Wayfinders?

Wayfinders of old used their knowledge of stars, swells, and currents to navigate thousands of kilometres of open ocean — without instruments, without charts, without margin for error. Wayfinder ESG uses modern tools and subject-matter expertise in OHS and environmental consulting to help organizations navigate a world of evolving regulations and rising compliance costs — and find their competitive edge.

The same principles that guided ancient navigators guide us: systematic observation, pattern recognition, and the discipline to act on what you find before conditions change.

Modern organizations face environments that are complex and unforgiving in exactly the same way: regulatory frameworks that shift without notice, liability exposures that compound in silence, and compliance costs that arrive as surprises rather than budgeted line items. The question is not whether a hazard exists. It is whether you found it before it found you.

Wayfinder is an occupational health, safety, and environmental consulting firm built on the belief that rigorous, structured expertise — applied with the same discipline that navigators brought to open-ocean crossings — is the most powerful tool for managing modern risk. We embed alongside client teams, map their complete exposure terrain, and deliver sequenced, costed outcomes with independently verifiable results.

Our practice spans three domains — OHS, Environmental & ESG, and Building Sciences — delivered under a single engagement model. No handoffs. No gaps between disciplines. One firm that knows where you are and can show you how to get where you need to be.
