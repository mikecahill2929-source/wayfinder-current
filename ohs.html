Practice 01

Occupational Health & Safety.

Five service lines built to eliminate regulatory exposure, protect field operations, and reduce the total cost of safety program failure.

Services

Six Service Lines. One Mandate.

01

Training & Competency Development

Regulatory competency gaps are the leading precursor to stop-work orders and lost-time claims — both of which carry costs your insurance premium reflects for years. We design and deliver role-specific training that closes those gaps before they become enforcement events, with records that survive a Ministry audit.

  • WHMIS / GHS & controlled products certification
  • JHSC co-chair & supervisor due diligence training
  • Custom new-hire OHS onboarding programs
02

Workers’ Compensation & Claims Management

A single lost-time incident can follow your WCB experience rating for three years — compounding insurance premiums and tender disqualifications long after the claim closes. We manage the full claims lifecycle, from first report through return-to-work, to protect your rate and keep workers in productive roles.

  • Claims advocacy & adjudicator liaison
  • Modified duty & return-to-work planning
  • WCB experience rating review & cost appeals
03

Health & Safety Management Systems

An OHS management system is the difference between a defensible safety record and an improvised one — regulators and insurers know the difference on inspection day. We build custom-fit programs from policy through procedure, designed for your operations and audit-ready from day one.

  • OHS policy, program & procedure development
  • Emergency response & business continuity planning
  • Contractor safety management & prequalification vetting
04

Workplace Audits & Compliance Inspections

Regulatory inspections don’t announce themselves, but their consequences compound immediately — stop-work orders, penalty assessments, and reputational exposure that affects your next bid. We conduct systematic gap assessments and hand you a prioritized remediation roadmap before the inspector does.

  • WorkSafeBC regulatory gap assessment
  • COR & ISO 45001 certification readiness audit
  • Site walkthrough with written corrective action plan
05

Risk & Hazard Assessments

Unassessed hazards don’t disappear — they become incidents, insurance events, and legal liabilities that arrive without warning. We systematically identify, evaluate, and control physical, chemical, and ergonomic hazard vectors across your operations before they reach your incident log or your insurer’s desk.

  • Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) & field risk surveys
  • Ergonomic evaluation & MSD prevention program
  • Chemical, physical & biological hazard control documentation
06

Incident Investigation & Root Cause Analysis

Internal investigations are rarely objective, and regulators and insurers both know it — which is why a credible third-party investigation is often the most cost-effective tool available after a serious incident. We produce defensible root cause analyses and regulatory-ready reports that withstand both enforcement and litigation scrutiny.

  • Third-party investigation & witness interviews
  • Root cause analysis (ICAM / bowtie methodology)
  • Regulatory reporting & preventive action plan
