Occupational

Protecting workers, satisfying regulators, and keeping operations running. Five integrated services under one engagement.

Services

What we do.

01.1
Workplace safety auditing
Systematic audit of your OHS program against WorkSafeBC and federal ESDC requirements. We identify gaps before regulators do — with a prioritized remediation plan, not just a report.
Key deliverables
Gap analysis against applicable legislation
Prioritized corrective action register
Regulatory citation risk rating
01.2
Pre-inspection simulation
We conduct a full regulatory inspection simulation before the inspector arrives. Walk the same path, ask the same questions, test the same records. Zero surprises on the day.
Key deliverables
Mock inspection report
Document readiness assessment
On-site remediation list
01.3
Field operations protection
Embedded safety advisory for active construction, maintenance, and high-hazard operations. We develop safe work procedures and stay on file through execution — not just at kickoff.
Key deliverables
Site-specific safe work procedures
JHA / FLHA templates
Incident response protocol
01.4
OHS & environmental training
WorkSafeBC-compliant OHS orientation, WHMIS 2015, supervisor safety leadership, and environmental awareness training. Delivered on-site or remotely with competency verification — not just attendance records.
Key deliverables
Training needs assessment
Customised training materials
Signed competency verification per participant
01.5
Workers' compensation management
Full-spectrum WCB and WorkSafeBC claims management — from first report through modified duty planning and cost control. We manage the administrative burden and protect your experience rating. Excludes legal proceedings.
Key deliverables
Claims intake and management
Modified duty program design
Experience rating analysis
