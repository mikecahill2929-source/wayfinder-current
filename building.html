Services

Practice 03 — Building sciences services.

03.1
Designated Substance Surveys
Comprehensive building assessments identifying hazardous materials before any renovation, demolition, or trade deployment.
  • Asbestos bulk sampling
  • Lead paint assessment
  • Mould identification
  • DSS report and substance registry
03.2
Contamination & Abatement Management
Project management of hazardous material abatement from contractor selection through clearance verification.
  • Abatement scope and tender
  • Contractor oversight
  • Air monitoring during works
  • Final clearance documentation
03.3
Indoor Air Quality & Clearance
IAQ baseline testing, occupant complaint investigations, and post-remediation clearance sampling.
  • Particulate and VOC sampling
  • HVAC and ventilation assessment
  • Post-abatement clearance testing
  • Occupant exposure report
03.4
Building Envelope Assessment
Building envelope performance review and moisture management advisory for existing and acquired assets.
Available 2027
  • Moisture intrusion investigation
  • Thermal performance review
  • Remediation sequencing
  • Capital planning advisory
03.5
Site Environmental Field Services
Phase I/II ESA support, soil and groundwater screening, and environmental baseline studies for site transactions.
Available 2027
  • Phase I ESA desktop review
  • Screening level field work
  • Regulatory liaison support
  • Transaction risk summary
