Services

Six Service Lines. One Building Sciences Partner.

03.1

Designated Substance Surveys

Before any renovation, demolition, or trade deployment, provincial regulations require a Designated Substance Survey that identifies asbestos, lead, silica, and other hazardous materials in the building. Without one, your contractor is working blind — and your organization carries the liability when a stop-work order lands or a worker files a compensation claim.

  • Asbestos bulk sampling & laboratory analysis
  • Lead, silica & designated substance assessment
  • DSS report with substance registry for WorkSafeBC compliance

03.2

Contamination & Abatement Management

Hazardous material abatement is one of the highest-liability phases of any renovation or demolition project — under-scoped contracts lead to cost overruns, failed clearance tests, and regulatory penalties that fall on the owner. We manage the full abatement lifecycle from contractor selection through air monitoring during works to signed clearance documentation that closes the owner’s liability file.

  • Abatement scope development, tender & contractor selection
  • Third-party air monitoring throughout active abatement works
  • Post-abatement clearance testing & sign-off documentation
Get in Touch →
03.3

Indoor Air Quality & Clearance Testing

Poor indoor air quality drives occupant health complaints, absenteeism, and — when ignored — WorkSafeBC investigations that expose building owners and operators to enforcement. We conduct IAQ baseline testing, investigate occupant complaints, and issue post-remediation clearance reports backed by accredited laboratory analysis and defensible methodology.

  • Particulate, VOC, CO&sub2; & biological contaminant sampling
  • HVAC performance & ventilation adequacy assessment
  • Post-abatement clearance testing & occupant exposure report
Get in Touch →
03.4

Mould Assessment & Remediation

Visible mould growth in a commercial building is a legal liability, an insurance event, and an occupant health crisis — often simultaneously. We conduct systematic mould investigations with air and surface sampling, develop remediation scopes that address moisture sources (not just surface growth), oversee contractor work, and issue clearance certificates that protect the owner post-project.

  • Mould inspection, air & surface sampling, lab analysis
  • Remediation scope addressing root moisture pathway
  • Contractor oversight & post-remediation clearance certificate
Get in Touch →
03.5

Building Envelope Assessment

Available 2027

Building envelope failures — moisture intrusion, thermal bridging, and cladding defects — are among the costliest defect categories an asset owner can inherit. We conduct performance reviews and moisture management assessments that identify failure modes and sequence capital remediation before water damage escalates to structural liability.

  • Moisture intrusion investigation & leak source mapping
  • Thermal performance review & energy loss assessment
  • Remediation sequencing & capital planning advisory
03.6

Site Environmental Field Services

Available 2027

Phase I/II Environmental Site Assessments provide the environmental liability picture that every acquisition, disposition, and lender due diligence process requires. We support site transactions with desktop ESA reviews, screening-level field work, and regulatory liaison that quantifies environmental risk exposure before it becomes a deal condition or a post-closing dispute.

  • Phase I ESA desktop review & site reconnaissance
  • Screening-level soil & groundwater field sampling
  • Transaction risk summary & regulatory liaison support
