Sectors

Where we work.

Eight sectors. Each with distinct OHS obligations, environmental compliance requirements, and ESG reporting pressures — often running simultaneously.

Industries

Sectors served.

01
Heavy construction
WorkSafeBC stop work orders, field safety supervision, and WCB cost management are existential risks on active sites. We deliver pre-inspection readiness programs, designated substance surveys before any structural alteration, site OHS leadership, and environmental baseline studies and spill prevention for brownfield and greenfield development.
02
Commercial real estate & strata
Building performance standards, GHG intensity penalties, and ESG disclosure requirements are reshaping asset values and lease negotiations. We manage BPS filings, GHG audits, energy retrofit sequencing, ESG reporting under GRI and GRESB, designated substance surveys for acquired or repositioned assets, and OHS programs for property management and strata corporations.
03
Industrial & manufacturing
Regulatory exposure in industrial environments compounds quickly — a single WorkSafeBC order or ISO 14001 nonconformance can halt production lines and trigger third-party audits. We deploy integrated OHS auditing, environmental compliance programs, ISO 14001 EMS implementation, GHG emissions accounting, and ESG reporting frameworks calibrated to industrial operating conditions.
04
Healthcare & long-term care
Healthcare facilities operate under overlapping OHS obligations — WHMIS, infection control, violence prevention, and workers' compensation management — alongside growing ESG reporting requirements tied to accreditation and public funding. We provide OHS program design, WCB management, IAQ assessment, GHG reporting, and ESG advisory for hospitals, clinics, and long-term care operators.
05
Supply chain & logistics
Multi-site, 24-hour operations create disproportionate WCB claims exposure, fatigue-related incident risk, and Scope 1 fleet emissions liability. We build enterprise-wide OHS systems, manage WCB cost containment, deliver fleet and warehouse safety programs, and produce Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions inventories and ESG reporting for logistics operators from last-mile to national distribution.
06
Energy & resources
Field operations in energy and resource extraction carry the highest severity-weighted risk profiles in any sector. We provide OHS field leadership, environmental compliance and spill response planning, Scope 1 and 2 GHG accounting, ISO 14001 EMS, net zero transition advisory, and ESG reporting for upstream, midstream, and downstream energy clients.
07
Government & public sector
Public sector organizations face intensifying disclosure obligations under federal and provincial sustainability reporting frameworks alongside internal OHS accountability to workers and unions. We deliver OHS program governance, environmental management systems, GRI-aligned sustainability reporting, and ESG advisory mandates for municipal, provincial, and Crown corporation clients.
08
Education
Post-secondary institutions manage complex multi-building campuses with aging infrastructure, significant designated substance exposure, and growing STARS and sustainability reporting obligations tied to endowment and accreditation. We provide campus OHS programs, IAQ and designated substance assessments for renovation and renewal projects, GHG reporting, and ESG advisory for colleges and universities.
