OHSStop-work order resolution · Commercial construction · British Columbia

Stop-work order lifted across three active construction sites in nine days

A mid-size commercial construction operator received a regional stop-work order across three active sites, citing inadequate safe work procedures for fall protection and confined space entry. Operations on all three sites were suspended pending regulatory compliance.

Approach

Mobilised on-site within 48 hours of engagement. Conducted a full gap analysis against WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation requirements across all cited trade classifications. Produced 24 task-specific safe work procedures covering fall protection, confined space entry, lock-out tag-out, and elevated work platforms. Ran a pre-reinspection readiness review with supervisors at each site before the regulatory return visit.

Environmental & ESG

EnvironmentalGHG penalty elimination · Commercial real estate · British Columbia

74% of GHG penalty exposure eliminated across a six-asset commercial office portfolio

A portfolio of six commercial office buildings was operating above applicable municipal GHG intensity thresholds, carrying cumulative assessed annual penalty exposure of approximately $180,000. The portfolio owner had received initial compliance notices and was approaching the first formal enforcement cycle.

Approach

Conducted a full Scope 1 and 2 GHG intensity audit across all six assets, collecting and cross-verifying twelve months of utility consumption data per property. Modelled every viable mechanical and building envelope improvement option. Sequenced a capital investment roadmap ordered strictly by penalty avoidance per dollar invested — prioritising the three assets with the highest per-dollar exposure first.
