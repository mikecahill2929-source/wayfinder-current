Practice 02

Environmental & ESG.

Six service lines spanning GHG compliance, ESG disclosure, environmental management systems, and net zero strategy.

Services

Seven Service Areas. One ESG Partner.

02.1

Air Emissions & Carbon Management

Atmospheric compliance and greenhouse gas programs that keep your operations ahead of federal and provincial thresholds — from stack emissions monitoring through full Scope 1, 2, and 3 GHG inventories using GHG Protocol methodology. We connect air quality compliance to your carbon reduction targets in one integrated program.

  • Stack & fugitive emissions inventory and reporting
  • Scope 1, 2 & 3 GHG inventory (GHG Protocol)
  • ECCC, BPS / BEPS & provincial regulatory filing
02.2

Water Quality & Stormwater

Surface water and groundwater compliance programs that protect discharge authorization and satisfy municipal stormwater requirements — from industrial effluent characterization to development-site SWPPP design. We keep your discharge permits clean and your monitoring records defensible under regulatory scrutiny.

  • Effluent characterization & discharge permit support
  • Stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) design
  • Water quality monitoring program & regulatory reporting
02.3

Energy Efficiency & Retrofit

Capital sequencing ranked by carbon reduction per dollar invested — not vendor recommendations. We identify utility incentive programs and build phased implementation roadmaps that survive board review and deliver measurable reductions against your energy intensity targets.

  • Energy audit across facilities & asset portfolio
  • Retrofit ROI prioritization & capital planning roadmap
  • Utility incentive identification (BC Hydro, FortisBC)
02.4

ISO 14001 Environmental Auditing

ISO 14001 certification opens doors to international supply chains, enterprise procurement programs, and lender ESG covenants that your competitors without it cannot access. We conduct gap assessments, build your Environmental Management System from policy through procedure, and prepare your organization for third-party certification audit.

  • ISO 14001 gap analysis & EMS development
  • Environmental aspects, impacts & legal register
  • Internal audit program & certification readiness review
02.5

ESG Reporting & Disclosure

GRI-aligned and GRESB-ready sustainability disclosures built for investor and lender audiences — from materiality assessment through stakeholder package design and annual report integration. We produce disclosures that hold up under institutional ESG due diligence and satisfy the frameworks your capital partners require.

  • GRI Standards reporting & materiality assessment
  • GRESB submission preparation & benchmarking
  • Investor-ready ESG disclosure package & data room
02.6

Net Zero & Climate Strategy

Science-based target setting and climate transition roadmap design — from SBTi target scoping through abatement pathway analysis to board-ready strategy decks that hold up under institutional investor due diligence. We connect emissions data to capital planning and procurement commitments your stakeholders can track.

  • SBTi target scoping & abatement pathway analysis
  • Carbon offset strategy & registry management
  • Board-level climate transition strategy deck
02.7

Materials, Recycling & Waste Reduction

Waste diversion programs that reduce landfill costs, satisfy extended producer responsibility requirements, and demonstrate circular economy commitments to customers and lenders who track your Scope 3 performance. We build material flow inventories, waste audits, and procurement criteria that keep waste out of your cost base and your ESG scorecard.

  • Waste audit & material flow inventory
  • Diversion program design & contractor vetting
  • EPR compliance & circular economy reporting
