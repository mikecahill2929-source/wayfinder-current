OHS Practice

Stop work order lifted in 9 days across three active construction sites

A multi-site contractor received simultaneous WorkSafeBC stop work orders across three active construction sites — 80+ workers affected — following a regulatory inspection that identified systemic OHS program failures. The orders required documented remediation before any operations could resume.

What we did

Wayfinder conducted an immediate gap assessment across all three sites, developed site-specific corrective action plans, produced the required documentation package, and managed the regulator communication process through to full clearance. All three sites were cleared and operations resumed within nine calendar days.

Environmental & ESG

$135K in first-year GHG penalties eliminated across a 400,000 sq ft portfolio

A six-asset commercial real estate portfolio faced compounding municipal GHG penalty exposure under the Toronto Better Buildings Partnership — with 74% of the exposure concentrated in two assets operating well above intensity thresholds.

What we did

Wayfinder conducted a full GHG audit across all six assets, identified the two high-exposure properties, developed a phased retrofit sequencing plan ranked by carbon reduction per dollar invested, and produced the BPS submission package. First-year penalty exposure was eliminated entirely, with a capital roadmap that prioritized the highest-return interventions first.
